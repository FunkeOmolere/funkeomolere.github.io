About Me

Where Compliance Meets Curiosity

I'm not going to pretend I have it all figured out. Here's the honest version.

I work in GRC — Governance, Risk and Compliance — and a while ago I got curious about whether I could actually build the tools I kept wishing existed. So I started. Slowly. Imperfectly. Googling things I probably should have known.

I'm new to vibe coding and GRC engineering, but I've been learning in public — building a live compliance tracker, exploring AI tools like Claude and Cursor, and figuring it out one commit at a time. The GRC Framework Update Tracker you'll see below? That's real work, built by someone who is still very much on the journey.

Outside of work, I'm a mum of three — ages 12, 9, and 3. I do school runs, bedtime stories, and compliance frameworks, sometimes all before 9am. I share that side of life on Instagram because I think it matters for people to see what working in tech can actually look like.
